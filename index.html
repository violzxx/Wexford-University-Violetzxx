The Wexford Ideal

An institution built around achievement.

Wexford is one of the country’s most prestigious private universities: selective, academically demanding, internationally respected, and visibly conscious of its own history. Students arrive exceptional. At Wexford, exceptional is merely the starting point.

The Central Idea

Wexford is beautiful because it is controlled.

Its lawns are maintained. Its halls endure. Its professors are accomplished. Its students are exceptional. Behind that immaculate appearance are thousands of ambitious people competing for grades, careers, recognition, affection, influence and control. Wexford remembers excellence. Wexford remembers failure. And at Wexford, everyone notices everything.
